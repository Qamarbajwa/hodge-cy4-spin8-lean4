# PERMANENT NO-GO LEDGER — HODGE CONJECTURE
*(Falsified hypotheses, known dead-ends, and disproved extensions stay permanently logged to prevent wasted research effort.)*

| No-Go ID | Claim / Hypothesis | Verdict | Reason / Disproving Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| **NO-GO-HODGE-001** | Integral Hodge Conjecture: $\operatorname{cl}(\mathcal{Z}^p(X)) = H^{2p}(X, \mathbb{Z}) \cap H^{p,p}(X)$ | **FALSIFIED / REJECTED** | Atiyah & Hirzebruch (1962) constructed 7-dimensional projective varieties with torsion $(p,p)$-classes that cannot be represented by algebraic cycles ($Sq^3$ obstruction). Totaro, Kollár, and Voisin later found torsion-free counterexamples on Calabi-Yau 3-folds. |
| **NO-GO-HODGE-002** | Extension of Hodge Conjecture to arbitrary compact Kähler manifolds | **FALSIFIED / REJECTED** | Claire Voisin (2002) proved the conjecture fails on general compact Kähler manifolds of dimension $\ge 4$ that do not have the rational homotopy type of any projective variety. Projectivity is mandatory. |
| **NO-GO-HODGE-003** | Spanning higher-codimension Hodge classes via Chern classes of vector bundles ($c_p(E)$) alone | **FALSIFIED / INSUFFICIENT** | Grothendieck and Mumford showed that the subring generated by Chern classes of algebraic vector bundles does not in general span the rational Chow ring or all rational algebraic cycles for $p \ge 2$. |
| **NO-GO-HODGE-004** | Topological cycle smoothing without complex structure | **FALSIFIED / REJECTED** | Smooth real submanifolds representing a homology class do not possess complex analytic tangent spaces; smooth topology alone cannot construct complex algebraic subvarieties. |
| **NO-GO-HODGE-005** | Claiming a general proof based solely on hypersurface verification | **INVALID REDUCTION** | Complete intersection hypersurfaces have trivial intermediate cohomology by the Lefschetz hyperplane theorem; testing only on hypersurfaces bypasses the entire difficulty of codimension $p \ge 2$. |
