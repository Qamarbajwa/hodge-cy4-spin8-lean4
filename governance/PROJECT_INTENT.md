# PROJECT INTENT — HODGE CONJECTURE RESEARCH LABORATORY
Status: OWNER_INTENT_CONFIRMED   Version: 1.0   Date: 2026-10-27

## 1. Identity
- **Purpose:** Rigorously investigate algebraic cycles, Hodge structures, and proposed resolution mechanisms for the Hodge Conjecture, establishing rock-solid mathematical understanding and verification without overstating what is proved.
- **Problem being investigated:** Determine whether every rational Hodge class on a smooth complex projective variety is algebraic, identifying the valid, open, false, or conditional mathematical bridges.
- **Governing Truth:** `NO_HODGE_PROOF = TRUE` unless and until an ironclad, peer-authenticated, and gap-free proof is constructed and verified against all known obstructions.

## 2. Desired Outcomes
1. **Source-authenticated theorem reconstruction:** Rigorous formulation of all Hodge-theoretic definitions, cycle class maps, and intermediate conjectures (Grothendieck standard conjectures, Hodge-Riemann bilinear relations, Griffiths intermediate Jacobians).
2. **Explicit dependency tracking:** Every lemma, assumption, and step tracked via strict ledger governance.
3. **Falsification-first auditing:** Immediate stress-testing of any proposed mechanism against known counterexamples (Atiyah-Hirzebruch, Voisin, Totaro) and structural roadblocks.
4. **Reproducible symbolic / computational testbeds:** Concrete verification of candidate mechanisms on known testbeds (Fermat varieties, abelian fourfolds, complete intersections).

## 3. Supported Scope
- Smooth projective complex algebraic varieties $X$ over $\mathbb{C}$.
- Rational cohomology $H^{2p}(X, \mathbb{Q})$ and $(p,p)$-forms $H^{p,p}(X)$.
- Algebraic cycles $\mathcal{Z}^p(X)_{\mathbb{Q}}$ and Chow groups $\operatorname{CH}^p(X)_{\mathbb{Q}}$.
- Analytic currents, Hodge filtration, Deligne cohomology, and Variations of Hodge Structures (VHS).

## 4. Definition of "Done"
A mathematical claim or lemma is considered accepted ONLY when recorded with exact definitions, explicit quantifiers, domain of validity, assumption minimization, independent adversarial review, and verification against known obstructions. Numerical or symbolic verification is never promoted to an analytic theorem without an explicit bridge.

## 5. Non-Goals / Forbidden Drift
- Implying or announcing a general proof of the Hodge Conjecture based on heuristics, analogies, or unverified steps.
- Promoting symbolic checks on low-degree hypersurfaces to general statements on arbitrary projective manifolds.
- Glossing over the $\mathbb{Z}$ vs $\mathbb{Q}$ distinction (ignoring torsion obstructions).
- Assuming compact Kähler manifolds satisfy the conjecture (ignoring Voisin's theorem).

## 6. Unacceptable Outcomes
- Fabricated or hallucinated citations, theorems, or computations.
- Proof drift (modifying the problem definition to make it trivial without formal ledger reclassification).
- Deleting or suppressing negative evidence, failed hypotheses, or counterexamples.

## 7. Zero-Tolerance Risk Policy
- False provenance of claims.
- Silent introduction of unjustified assumptions.
- Promotion of numerics/symbolics to continuum/general geometric theorems.
