## 1. Project identity

- Project name: Riemann Hypothesis CCM/CvS Research Program
- One-sentence purpose: Rigorously investigate RH-relevant Connes–Consani–Moscovici / Connes–van Suijlekom spectral mechanisms and produce reliable mathematical understanding without overstating what is proved.
- Intended stakeholders: project owner, mathematical researchers, independent reviewers, and future maintainers of the research record.
- Problem being solved: determine which finite, continuum, analytic, and arithmetic bridges in the CCM/CvS program are valid, open, false, or conditionally established.

## 2. Desired outcomes

1. Source-authenticated theorem reconstruction and explicit dependency tracking.
2. Reproducible scientific computation with candidate-bound evidence, negative controls, and preserved failure history.
3. Governed development of conjectures, lemmas, counterexamples, and conditional proofs, with `NO_RH_PROOF = TRUE` unless genuinely new proof evidence changes that state.

## 3. Declared supported scope

- Source-authenticated mathematical and scientific analysis of the RH/CCM/CvS program.
- Deterministic local numerical computation and reproducible artifact generation.
- Governed conjecture, proof, counterexample, theorem-gap, and regression work.
- Rigorous negative results and falsification of unsupported pathways.
- Independent challenge and evidence-based classification of mathematical claims.

## 4. Definition of project-level done

- A research claim is recorded with exact definitions, assumptions, scope, evidence dimensions, candidate identity, limitations, and reproducible provenance.
- Historical results, failures, proof errors, and open gaps remain discoverable.
- No claim is promoted from computation to proof, from finite section to continuum, or from local evidence to external/public readiness without the required bridge.

## 5. Non-goals / forbidden drift

The project is explicitly not trying to:

- manufacture or imply an RH proof;
- promote numerical evidence into a theorem;
- present finite-section positivity or convergence as a continuum theorem without the missing analytic bridge;
- conceal failed pathways, wrong-operator evidence, source corrections, or proof regressions;
- maximize proof or claim count at the expense of correctness;
- alter protected public-oracle fixtures or sealed clean-room evidence to improve a result.

## 6. Unacceptable outcomes

- fabricated, synthetic, or candidate-mismatched evidence represented as authentic;
- wrong-operator results promoted as CCM/CvS truth;
- source misquotation or assumption drift;
- false independent-verification claims;
- unsupported “RH solved” or equivalent language;
- deletion or laundering of negative evidence;
- governance changes used to bypass dependency, risk, evidence, or reserved-authority gates.

## 7. Business constraints

- Budget/spend envelope: no external spend or publication exposure is authorized by this intent; local bounded computation is permitted.
- Commercial/vendor constraints: preserve the authenticated `connes-cvs == 0.3.1` oracle and declared source provenance.
- Customer exposure constraints: none; this is not a customer-facing product.
- Timeline/milestones: technical sequencing is governed by the roadmap and evidence, not ceremonial deadlines.

## 8. Risk tolerance

- Critical outcomes that must be zero-tolerance: fabricated proof status, false evidence provenance, candidate/evidence mismatch, loss of negative history, and accidental public/external claim exposure.
- Degradation that may be tolerated: incomplete automation, bounded numerical precision limits, and explicitly declared conditional or unverified states.
- Experimental areas allowed: local mathematical analysis and reversible repository artifact mutation up to the active T3 ceiling.
- Production/customer exposure restrictions: publication, external release, and material exposure remain separately reserved actions.

## 9. Legal/privacy/compliance boundaries

- No legal conclusion is inferred by this document.
- Avoid exposing secrets or unnecessary personal data in research artifacts.
- Legal, privacy, publication, and external-claim policy decisions remain reserved to the owner/counsel where applicable.

## 10. Quality priorities

Correctness and truthfulness; reproducibility; mathematical precision; provenance; negative-evidence preservation; independent challenge; maintainability; clarity; and only then speed or cost.

## 11. Supported environments / product expectations

- Local Windows 11 x64 research workspace.
- Python 3.12.10 with the locked scientific stack recorded in `PROJECT_PROFILE.md` and `ENVIRONMENT_LOCK.md`.
- Authenticated public `connes-cvs == 0.3.1` oracle and declared local fixture regime where applicable.
- External publication or deployment environments are not implicitly supported by local evidence.

## 12. Reserved owner decisions

- material change to research purpose, supported scope, non-goals, or scientific epistemic ceiling;
- publication or external exposure of a mathematical claim;
- business/spend/provider policy beyond the current envelope;
- legal/privacy/compliance judgments;
- destructive or irreversible production action;
- deployment/publication authorization for an already technically eligible candidate.

Project-specific additions:

- changing `NO_RH_PROOF = TRUE` requires actual proof-grade evidence and explicit scientific-governance review, not confidence or generic approval;
- changing the protected oracle, sealed clean-room evidence, or mathematical constitution is a separately governed high-consequence change.

## 13. Standing autonomous technical mandate

Once this intent is bound, engineering/research may autonomously derive and execute dependency-satisfied technical architecture, task decomposition, tests, assurance contracts, repairs, and evidence work inside the boundaries above. Routine owner technical certification is not required.

This project-level invariant remains active until changed by actual proof-grade evidence through the preserved mathematical claim lifecycle. The generic software release lifecycle is orthogonal to that scientific lifecycle.
