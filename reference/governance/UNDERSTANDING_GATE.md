# UNDERSTANDING GATE PROTOCOL
**Governing Standard:** `MATHEMATICAL_RESEARCH_CONSTITUTION_v1.0 §11`  

---

## 1. The Three-Level Explanation Requirement

No verified theorem, candidate lemma, or proof step may be promoted to `HUMAN_UNDERSTOOD` or `CANONICALIZED` without meeting the 3-Level Understanding Standard:

### Level 1: One-Sentence Core Mechanism
- A single, dense, precise sentence explaining the exact mathematical mechanism driving the theorem.
- *Forbidden:* "The theorem follows from standard spectral theory."
- *Required:* "The even parity sector has strictly lower ground energy than the odd sector because the non-local arithmetic prime summation is positive on symmetric convolution squares, acting as an attractive potential that selectively depresses even modes."

### Level 2: One-Page Proof Architecture
- A structured 1-page document detailing:
  1. Primary strategy (e.g. variational perturbation, contour integration, spectral comparison).
  2. The exact load-bearing lemma carrying the non-trivial burden.
  3. Key intermediate estimates with explicit constants.
  4. Where and why classical or trivial techniques break down.

### Level 3: Rigorous Deductive Proof
- Complete, fully justified, step-by-step mathematical proof where every inference cites a verified lemma, primary theorem, or elementary identity with zero gap.

---

## 2. Mandatory Structural Questions

Every Level 2 understanding dossier must answer:
1. **Where is the real difficulty?** (Identify the exact step where trivial bounds fail).
2. **Why does the proof work?** (What structural property of the operator/function enables the deduction?).
3. **Which assumptions are essential vs proof artifacts?**
4. **What exact change in hypotheses would produce a counterexample?**
5. **How does this result differ conceptually from previous literature?**

If these questions cannot be answered, the theorem status is formally recorded as:
$$\boxed{\mathbf{STATUS = VERIFIED\_BUT\_NOT\_YET\_UNDERSTOOD}}$$
