# CANONICALIZATION STAGE PROTOCOL
**Governing Standard:** `MATHEMATICAL_RESEARCH_CONSTITUTION_v1.0 §15`  

---

## 1. Principle

$$\mathbf{FIRST\_PROOF \neq CANONICAL\_THEOREM}$$

A verified and assumption-minimized result enters the Canonicalization Stage to determine the natural, coordinate-free, and general formulation of the mathematical discovery.

---

## 2. Canonicalization Checklist

1. **Parameter-Free Invariance:** Can explicit artificial parameters (e.g. interval truncation, coordinate dilation factors) be absorbed into an intrinsic geometric or operator-theoretic statement?
2. **Abstract Operator Generalization:** Does the theorem hold for a broader class of self-adjoint semibounded operators or spectral triples?
3. **Unification with Neighboring Theorems:** Does the result subsume previous partial lemmas (e.g. bridging CCM-A1 and CCM-A2 into a unified Parity-Spectral Theorem)?
4. **Permanent Canonical Registry:** Once certified, the theorem is added to `CANONICAL_PROJECT_THEOREMS.md` and referenced by unique canonical identifier.
