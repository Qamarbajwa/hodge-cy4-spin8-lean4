# Next governed proof obligations

The finite LDL and defined spatial component are usable evidence-building tools.
They do not validate the complete historical operator or certify an RH interval.

1. Freeze one canonical logarithmic Weil form and normalization. Explain the
   negative spatial sign in the pre-L7 construction and positive sign in L9-L11.
   Derive the exact finite/complement block identity before trusting either.
2. Enclose Archimedean and pole entries with validated integration and special
   functions. Include all quadrature, truncation, normalization and rounding
   errors. Exact integration has removed spatial polynomial quadrature, but
   does not remove these other obligations.
3. Prove the complement floor and cross-block estimate on the actual form domain.
   Increasing T or a positive compressed matrix cannot replace this proof.
4. Combine uniform spatial cell boxes with uniformly enclosed remaining blocks.
   Adaptive subdivision may tighten boxes only after their inclusion theorem
   is established. Cover closed cells and activation endpoints, including L7-L8.
5. Execute the full thirteen-probe suite on the first complete enclosed interval.
   Current probes cover only specific finite or spatial components. Basis,
   quadrature, T and full Schur-method comparisons remain unavailable; they are
   not counted as PASS. Independent full reconstruction must avoid the same
   analytic builder.
6. Freeze that interval proof object, then distinct scientific review and
   technical acceptance may assess it. Only a genuinely accepted contiguous
   chain permits a frontier update.

Formalization assessment: exact recurrence and Bernstein convex-hull lemmas are
small practical proof-assistant targets. Current Python exact arithmetic is not
Lean/Isabelle/Coq certification; no formal-check status is claimed. Formalization
would not settle the missing form identity or analytic estimates by itself.

Cost record: current v2 benchmarks are small component demonstrations. They do
not establish feasibility for historical 50–205 mode matrices. Polynomial degree,
rational integer growth, full coupling dimensions, interval dependency and
precision should be recorded before scaling. The spatial discrepancy diagnostic
shows numerical integration error can dominate a stored margin; it does not
identify true spectral deterioration or prove almost orthogonality.

Preserve negative results. The hourly thread heartbeat may pursue these bounded
stages; it does not turn this list into an automatically proved theorem.
