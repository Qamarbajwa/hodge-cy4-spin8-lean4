# Deep Investigation: Abelian Fourfolds of Weil Type (The Millennium Counterexample Frontier)

*Mathematical anatomy of the prime candidate for a rational counterexample to the Hodge Conjecture.*

---

## 1. What is an Abelian Variety of Weil Type?

Let $A = \mathbb{C}^4 / \Lambda$ be a complex abelian fourfold ($\dim_{\mathbb{C}}(A) = 4$), where $\Lambda \cong \mathbb{Z}^8$ is a lattice of rank 8.
Let $K = \mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{-d})$ be an imaginary quadratic number field ($d \in \mathbb{Z}^+$ square-free).

### 1.1 The Weil Condition
$A$ is said to be of **Weil type** with respect to $K$ if:
1. $K$ embeds into the rational endomorphism algebra:
   $$K \hookrightarrow \operatorname{End}^0(A) = \operatorname{End}(A) \otimes_{\mathbb{Z}} \mathbb{Q}$$
2. The complex tangent representation of $K$ on the Lie algebra $T_0(A) \cong \mathbb{C}^4$ is of **balanced signature** $(2, 2)$:
   $$T_0(A) \cong V_{\sqrt{-d}}^{\oplus 2} \oplus V_{-\sqrt{-d}}^{\oplus 2}$$
   where $V_{\pm \sqrt{-d}}$ are the 1-dimensional eigenspaces corresponding to the two embeddings of $K$ into $\mathbb{C}$.

## 2. The Weil Classes $W(A) \subset H^4(A, \mathbb{Q})$

Let $H_1(A, \mathbb{Q}) \cong \Lambda \otimes \mathbb{Q}$ be the 8-dimensional $\mathbb{Q}$-vector space. Under the action of $K$, $H_1(A, \mathbb{Q})$ is a 4-dimensional vector space over $K$.

### 2.1 The Construction (André Weil, 1977)
In the 4th exterior power:
$$H^4(A, \mathbb{Q}) \cong \bigwedge^4 H^1(A, \mathbb{Q})$$
Weil observed that the highest exterior power over $K$:
$$\bigwedge_K^4 H^1(A, \mathbb{Q})$$
defines a canonical **2-dimensional rational subspace**:
$$W(A) \subset H^4(A, \mathbb{Q})$$
called the **space of Weil classes**.

### 2.2 Why the Weil Classes are Hodge Classes
Under the complex Hodge decomposition:
$$H^4(A, \mathbb{C}) = H^{4,0} \oplus H^{3,1} \oplus H^{2,2} \oplus H^{1,3} \oplus H^{0,4}$$
The balanced signature $(2, 2)$ of the tangent representation forces the exterior power $\bigwedge_K^4$ to have weight $(2, 2)$:
$$W(A) \otimes \mathbb{C} \subset H^{2,2}(A)$$
Therefore, every class $w \in W(A)$ is a **genuine rational Hodge class**:
$$W(A) \subset H^4(A, \mathbb{Q}) \cap H^{2,2}(A) = \operatorname{Hdg}^4(A)$$

## 3. The Algebraic Gap: Why Divisors Cannot Explain $W(A)$

For a **very general** abelian fourfold of Weil type in its 4-dimensional moduli family:
- The endomorphism algebra is strictly $K$: $\operatorname{End}^0(A) \cong K$.
- The Néron-Severi group has minimal rank (Picard number $\rho(A) = 1$ or $2$).
- The subspace of $H^4(A, \mathbb{Q})$ generated by the intersection of divisor classes:
  $$\operatorname{Sym}^2 \operatorname{NS}(A)_{\mathbb{Q}} \subset H^{2,2}(A)$$
  has dimension at most $1$ (or $2$).

### 3.1 The Orthogonality Theorem
Under the cup product / Poincaré intersection pairing on $H^4(A, \mathbb{Q})$:
$$W(A) \perp \operatorname{Sym}^2 \operatorname{NS}(A)_{\mathbb{Q}}$$
The 2-dimensional space of Weil classes is **strictly orthogonal** to all products of divisor classes!

$$\dim\left( W(A) \cap \operatorname{span}_{\mathbb{Q}}\{ [D_1] \smile [D_2] \} \right) = 0$$

> [!CRITICAL]
> **The Core Obstruction:**
> On a general Weil fourfold, the Weil classes **CANNOT be produced by intersecting algebraic divisors**. 
> If the Hodge Conjecture is true for $A$, there must exist an **exotic algebraic surface $S \subset A$** whose fundamental class $[S]$ lies in $W(A)$, yet $S$ is completely disconnected from the divisor geometry of $A$.

## 4. The Counterexample Hypothesis

1. **The Question:** On a *very general* abelian fourfold of Weil type $A$ (outside the Schoen-Prym locus), does there exist ANY algebraic surface $S \subset A$?
2. **If YES:** The Hodge conjecture survives this supreme stress-test, and one must discover a new, non-divisor geometric construction for $S$.
3. **If NO:** **THE HODGE CONJECTURE IS FALSE OVER $\mathbb{Q}$.**
   The Weil class $w \in W(A)$ is an explicit, non-zero rational $(2,2)$ Hodge class with zero algebraic cycles to account for it.
