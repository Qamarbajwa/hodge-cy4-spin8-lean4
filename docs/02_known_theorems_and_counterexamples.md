# Known Theorems and Counterexamples

## 1. Solved Cases

### 1.1 Lefschetz (1,1) Theorem ($p=1$)
* **Theorem:** For any smooth complex projective variety $X$, $\operatorname{Hdg}^2(X) = \operatorname{cl}(\mathcal{Z}^1(X)_{\mathbb{Q}})$.
* **Mechanism:** 
  The exponential sheaf sequence on $X$:
  $$0 \longrightarrow \mathbb{Z} \xrightarrow{2\pi i} \mathcal{O}_X \xrightarrow{\exp} \mathcal{O}_X^\times \longrightarrow 0$$
  induces the long exact sequence in sheaf cohomology:
  $$\cdots \to H^1(X, \mathcal{O}_X^\times) \xrightarrow{c_1} H^2(X, \mathbb{Z}) \to H^2(X, \mathcal{O}_X) \to \cdots$$
  $H^1(X, \mathcal{O}_X^\times) \cong \operatorname{Pic}(X)$ is the Picard group of isomorphism classes of holomorphic line bundles.
  A class $\alpha \in H^2(X, \mathbb{Z})$ maps to zero in $H^2(X, \mathcal{O}_X) \cong H^{0,2}(X)$ if and only if it is of type $(1,1)$.
  Therefore, every integral (and rational) $(1,1)$ class is the first Chern class $c_1(\mathcal{L})$ of a line bundle $\mathcal{L}$, which is represented by a divisor (codimension 1 algebraic cycle).

### 1.2 Codimension $n-1$ Cycles ($p=n-1$)
* By the **Hard Lefschetz Theorem**:
  $$L^{n-2}: H^2(X, \mathbb{Q}) \xrightarrow{\sim} H^{2n-2}(X, \mathbb{Q})$$
  combined with Poincaré duality, Hodge classes of degree $2n-2$ are generated by intersections of divisors with powers of the hyperplane class, resolving $p = n - 1$.

### 1.3 Varieties of Dimension $\le 3$
* Dimension 1 (curves): $p=0, 1$ (trivial / points).
* Dimension 2 (surfaces): $p=0, 1, 2$ ($p=1$ is Lefschetz (1,1); $p=2$ are 0-cycles/points).
* Dimension 3 (threefolds): $p=0, 1, 2, 3$ ($p=1$ by Lefschetz; $p=2$ is $n-1$, solved by Hard Lefschetz; $p=3$ are points).
* **Conclusion:** The first genuinely open case is $p=2$ on a variety of dimension 4 (algebraic surfaces in a 4-fold).

### 1.4 Special Classes of Varieties
The Hodge conjecture is known for:
- Fermat varieties of certain degrees (Shioda, Ran).
- Abelian varieties of CM-type in certain configurations.
- Uniruled threefolds and certain rationally connected varieties.
- Complete intersections of general type (via Noether-Lefschetz type theorems).

## 2. False Extensions & Counterexamples (Boundaries of the Conjecture)

### 2.1 The Integral Hodge Conjecture Fails
* Hodge originally conjectured:
  $$\operatorname{cl}(\mathcal{Z}^p(X)) = H^{2p}(X, \mathbb{Z}) \cap H^{p,p}(X)$$
* **Atiyah & Hirzebruch (1962):** Disproved using topological K-theory and Steenrod operations ($Sq^3$). They constructed a smooth projective variety with non-trivial torsion classes of type $(p,p)$ that cannot come from algebraic cycles.
* **Totaro, Kollár, Voisin:** Found torsion-free counterexamples to the integral Hodge conjecture on Calabi-Yau 3-folds and uniruled 3-folds for 1-cycles.
* **Key Takeaway:** Any general proof MUST use rational coefficients $\mathbb{Q}$ and cannot purely rely on integral topological operations without division.

### 2.2 Compact Complex Manifolds (Non-Projective / Non-Kähler)
* The conjecture is false on general compact complex manifolds.
* **Voisin (2002):** Constructed compact Kähler manifolds of dimension $\ge 4$ that do not have the rational homotopy type of any projective variety and on which the analogue of the Hodge conjecture fails.
* **Key Takeaway:** Projectivity (existence of an ample line bundle / embedding into $\mathbb{P}^N$) is strictly required; Kähler alone is insufficient in dimension $\ge 4$.
