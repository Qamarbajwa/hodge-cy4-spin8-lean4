\begin{abstract}
The Hodge Conjecture posits that every rational Hodge class on a smooth complex projective variety is algebraic, represented by a formal rational linear combination of algebraic subvarieties. While the classical Lefschetz theorem settles the conjecture in codimension one through the exponential sheaf sequence, higher codimension presents formidable geometric and arithmetic obstructions. In particular, general methods stall on two fundamental testbeds: smooth Calabi--Yau fourfolds in projective five-space, where algebraic surfaces encounter normal bundle obstructions under deformation, and abelian sixfolds of Mumford type, where the absence of auxiliary lower-dimensional varieties defeats traditional secant-sheaf constructions.

On the Calabi--Yau testbed, the infinitesimal variation of Hodge structure translates the geometry of embedded surfaces into commutative algebra via Griffiths residues and Macaulay inverse systems. On the Fermat sextic fourfold, the middle primitive cohomology decomposes under discrete cyclotomic symmetries into three geometric bands with zero residual remainder, corresponding to planes, joins of curves, and primitive surface joins. When moving away from the Fermat point, individually obstructed planes recombine along shared curves into smooth quadric surfaces whose net normal obstruction vanishes. The covariant transition determinant of Wiebe, Scheja, and Storch provides an algebraic invariant that follows these surfaces under continuous deformation, recovering their defining ideals through Jacobian ring colon operations.

On the abelian sixfold testbed, the Mumford--Tate group acts via the irreducible spinor representation of the orthogonal Lie algebra in eight dimensions. Because the endomorphism algebra of this half-spinor space is trivial, no auxiliary abelian threefolds or reflexive secant sheaves exist. Instead, the middle Hodge classes correspond to zero-weight configurations in the fourth exterior power of the spinor representation. The exceptional triality automorphism of the twenty-four-cell root system governs these weight configurations, embedding the vector and half-spinor spaces into the even Clifford algebra. Symmetries of the Weyl group systematically organize these classes into orthogonal chiral representations and connect them to motivated and absolute Hodge cycles.

This work presents a comprehensive computational investigation and formal verification suite exploring both testbeds. Using exact rational arithmetic, finite-field linear algebra, and modular reconstruction algorithms, we compute explicit multiplication ranks, verify obstruction cancellation across complete deformation slices, and catalog the combinatorial geometry of spinor weight orbits. In addition, forty-three finite combinatorial and degree-counting identities are verified constructively in core Lean without external mathematical libraries. While these results establish exact local and representation-theoretic invariants, the global Clay Millennium Hodge Conjecture remains strictly open: determining effective degree bounds for cycles across general deformations and constructing global algebraic cycles from motivated correspondences remain central unsolved problems.
\end{abstract}

\[
\cl_X : \CH^p(X)_{\mathbb{Q}} \longrightarrow \Hdg^{2p}(X) := H^{2p}(X, \mathbb{Q}) \cap H^{p,p}(X)
\]
is surjective. This article presents reproducible exact rational and modular ($\mathbb{F}_p$) computational experiments alongside decidable finite combinatorial Lean~4 checks across two structured testbeds: (i) the infinitesimal variation of Hodge structure (IVHS) and Artinian Gorenstein Macaulay inverse systems \cite{CarlsonGriffiths1980,Voisin2002} on smooth sextic Calabi--Yau fourfolds $X_4 \subset \mathbb{P}^5$, and (ii) the $\mathrm{Spin}(8)$ / $D_4$ Clifford--triality combinatorics on Mumford-type abelian sixfolds \cite{Mumford1969,KugaSatake1967,Deligne1982}.

\subsection{Prior Computational and Formalization Work}
Computational approaches to algebraic cycles and Hodge theory have a rich history in computer algebra. Classical computer algebra systems---notably \textsc{Macaulay2} \cite{GraysonStillman}, \textsc{Singular} \cite{DGPS}, and \textsc{Magma} \cite{BosmaCannonPlayoust}---provide standard algorithms for Gr{\"o}bner bases, syzygies, and Hilbert functions of Artinian rings. However, on sextic fourfolds in $\mathbb{P}^5$, direct symbolic elimination encounters prohibitive complexity: the Milnor number is $\mu = 15,625$, and the Jacobian multiplication matrices have size $1,751 \times 426$, making general rational Gr{\"o}bner basis calculations intractable without specialized exploitation of cyclotomic and representation-theoretic symmetries.

In interactive theorem proving, an exhaustive live audit across global Lean~4 repositories (\texttt{mathlib4}, \texttt{formal-conjectures}, and \texttt{FLT}) reveals that formalization of Hodge theory remains in its infancy:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em]
\item \textbf{The Hodge Conjecture and Cycle Class Map:} Neither the Hodge Conjecture nor the cycle class map $\cl_X : \CH^p(X)_{\mathbb{Q}} \to \Hdg^{2p}(X)$ is formalized in \texttt{mathlib4}, \texttt{formal-conjectures}, or the FLT repository (zero code hits across all three).
\item \textbf{Commutative Algebra and Macaulay Systems:} While \texttt{mathlib4} contains foundational theory for Noetherian rings, depth, and Cohen--Macaulay rings, Artinian Gorenstein socle duality, Macaulay inverse systems, and apolarity pairings remain unformalized.
\item \textbf{Gotzmann's Persistence and Fermat Hypersurfaces:} Gotzmann's regularity and persistence theorem, as well as the Shioda--Aoki character decomposition of Fermat hypersurfaces, have zero formalizations in the Lean ecosystem.
\item \textbf{Clifford Algebras and Number Theory:} Mathlib provides robust formalizations of Clifford algebras and the Chinese Remainder Theorem for rings, but $\mathrm{Spin}(8)$ triality outer automorphisms and Wang's modular rational reconstruction algorithm remain unformalized.
\end{itemize}
Consequently, our Lean formalization does not attempt to formalize the global category of schemes or mixed Hodge structures; rather, it isolates forty-three decidable finite combinatorial and algebraic invariants, verifying them constructively with empty axioms in core Lean~4.

\section{Experiment A: 4-Band Shioda--Lefschetz Closure and Tangential Leibniz Transport}
\label{sec:thmA}
% ==============================================================================

Let $X_4 = \{F = 0\} \subset \mathbb{P}^5$ be a smooth sextic fourfold, with Jacobian ideal $J(F) = (\partial_0 F, \dots, \partial_5 F) \subset S = \mathbb{Q}[x_0,\dots,x_5]$ and Artinian Gorenstein Jacobian ring $R(F) = S / J(F)$ of socle degree $s = 6(6 - 2) = 24$ and Milnor number $\mu = 5^6 = 15,625$. By the Griffiths residue isomorphism \cite{CarlsonGriffiths1980},
\[
R^{6q}(F) \cong H^{4-q,q}_{\mathrm{prim}}(X_4, \mathbb{C}), \qquad \dim R^{(0,6,12,18,24)}(F) = (1, 426, 1751, 426, 1),
\]
giving $h^{2,2}_{\mathrm{total}} = 1,752$ and $b_4 = 2,606$.

\begin{proposition}[Experiment A: 4-Band Closure, Cyclotomic Wavepackets, and $407/407$ Tangential Transport]
\label{thm:A}
Let $F_0(x) = \frac{1}{6}\sum_{i=0}^5 x_i^6$ be the Fermat sextic and $F_0^{\mathrm{signed}}(x) = x_0^6 - x_3^6 + x_1^6 - x_4^6 + x_2^6 - x_5^6$.
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\roman*)]
\item \textbf{4-Band Shioda--Lefschetz Closure:} Under the diagonal character group $(\mathbb{Z}/6\mathbb{Z})^6$ \cite{Shioda1979}, every basis monomial $x^a \in R^{12}(F_0)$ ($a \in \{0,1,2,3,4\}^6$, $\sum a_i = 12$) satisfies Shioda's Galois norm $\sum_{i=0}^5 (t(a_i+1) \bmod 6) = 18$ for $t \in \{1,5\}$, and partitions disjointly across all $18$ unordered partitions as
\[
1,751 = \underbrace{1,001}_{\text{Band 1: } 2+2+2} + \underbrace{630}_{\text{Band 2: } 3+3} + \underbrace{120}_{\text{Band 3: } 2+4\text{ join}} + \underbrace{0}_{\text{Band 4: residual}}.
\]
Here Band~1 lies in the span of projective $2$-planes, Band~2 comes from projective joins of Fermat curves, and Band~3 consists of two partitions $(4,2,2,2,1,1)$ ($60$ modes) and $(3,3,2,2,2,0)$ ($60$ modes) factoring via $\mathrm{Join}(X^0_6, C_{\mathrm{Lef}})$ from $R^8(F_{\mathbb{P}^3}) \cong H^{1,1}_{\mathrm{prim}}(X^2_6)$ ($\dim = 85$).
\item \textbf{Cyclotomic $2$-Plane Wavepacket:} The $2$-plane $\Pi_{\mathrm{true}} = V(x_0-x_3, x_1-x_4, x_2-x_5) \subset \{F_0^{\mathrm{signed}}=0\}$ has dual apolar class
\[
\Lambda_{\Pi_{\mathrm{true}}} = \prod_{k=0}^{2} \left(\sum_{a=0}^{4} x_k^{4-a} x_{k+3}^a\right) \in R^{12}(F_0^{\mathrm{signed}})
\]
supported on $5^3 = 125$ pure Band-1 monomials. Its graded multiplication maps $M^{(m)}_{\Lambda_{\Pi_{\mathrm{true}}}} : R^m \to R^{12+m}$ ($m = 1, \dots, 6$) have exact ranks $(3, 6, 10, 15, 18, 19)$ (sum $+ 1 = 72$, Gotzmann persistence $G=1$ \cite{Gotzmann1978}), recovering $\Ann_{R^1}(\Lambda_{\Pi_{\mathrm{true}}}) = \langle x_0-x_3, x_1-x_4, x_2-x_5 \rangle$.
\item \textbf{$407/407$ Signed Tangential Leibniz Closure:} Writing $D_k^+ = \partial_k + \partial_{k+3}$, all $\dim \ker(M^{(6)}_{\Lambda_{\Pi_{\mathrm{true}}}}) = 407$ tangent directions lift to $I_{\Pi_{\mathrm{true}},6}$, obey $D_k^+(\tilde{u}) \in I_{\Pi_{\mathrm{true}},5}$ ($0$ unlifted out of $407$), and close jointly under generator--shadow transport $(g_k + t\,\delta g_k)(\Lambda_0 + t\,\Lambda_1) \equiv 0 \pmod{J(F_t), t^2}$ with doubled $2 \times 2$ dual-number ranks $(6, 12, 20, 30, 36, 38)$.
\end{enumerate}
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}[Computational Justification]
Part~(i) follows from exact enumeration of the $1,751$ monomials of degree $12$ bounded by $a_i \le 4$: $1,001$ admit one of the $15$ perfect matchings satisfying $a_i+a_j = 4$; $630$ residual modes admit a $3$-subset satisfying $(a_i+a_j+a_k+3) \equiv 0 \pmod 6$; and all remaining $120$ modes admit a pair with $a_i+a_j = 4$. Part~(ii) holds by telescoping: $(x_k - x_{k+3})\sum_{a=0}^4 x_k^{4-a}x_{k+3}^a = x_k^5 - x_{k+3}^5 \in J(F_0^{\mathrm{signed}})$, while on the quotient $\mathbb{Q}[z_0,z_1,z_2]/(z_0^5,z_1^5,z_2^5)$ the images form a linearly independent basis of dimensions $(3,6,10,15,18,19)$ in degrees $1 \dots 6$. For Part~(iii), tangential derivations $D_k^+ = \partial_k + \partial_{k+3}$ annihilate $y_j = x_j - x_{j+3}$ ($D_k^+(y_j) = 0$) and hence preserve $I_{\Pi_{\mathrm{true}}} = (y_0,y_1,y_2)$ by the Leibniz rule. Exact rational RREF over $\mathbb{Q}$ verifies $407/407$ lifting on $F_0^{\mathrm{signed}}$ versus $359/407$ ($48$ unlifted normal directions) on the unsigned Fermat variety.
\end{proof}

\section{Experiment B: Rank--Nullity Lower Bounds, 9-Partition Gorenstein Duality, and \texorpdfstring{$K=5$}{K=5} Coverage}
\label{sec:thmB}
% ==============================================================================

For a fixed deformation tangent $u \in R^6(F_0)$ ($\dim = 426$), consider the Carlson--Griffiths first-order IVHS contraction $M_u : R^{12}(F_0) \to R^{18}(F_0)$, $M_u(\lambda) = u \cdot \lambda \bmod J(F_0)_{18}$. Its kernel $\ker(M_u)$ is the space of Hodge classes whose Hodge locus remains flat along $u$ to first order.

\begin{proposition}[Experiment B: Kernel Bounds, Moduli Rank Extremes, and $K=5$ Coverage]
\label{thm:B}
On the Fermat sextic Jacobian ring $R(F_0)$:
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\roman*)]
\item \textbf{Universal Band Kernel Lower Bounds:} For \emph{every} $u \in R^6(F_0)$ and subspace $V \subseteq R^{12}(F_0)$ of dimension $d_V$, rank--nullity forces $\dim \ker(M_u|_V) \ge d_V - 426$. In particular, unconditionally for all $u \in R^6(F_0)$:
\[
\dim \ker(M_u) \ge 1,325, \qquad \dim \ker(M_u|_{\mathrm{Band\,1}}) \ge 575, \qquad \dim \ker(M_u|_{\mathrm{Band\,2}}) \ge 204.
\]
\item \textbf{9-Partition Gorenstein Moduli Rank Census:} Partitioning the $426$ basis monomials $x^b \in R^6(F_0)$ into their $9$ unordered exponent types, Gorenstein duality $a'_i = (4-b_i)-a_i$ matches multiplication ranks with dual box-counts identically:
\[
\rk(M_{x^b}) \in [155, 336], \qquad \dim \ker(M_{x^b}) \in [1,415, 1,596],
\]
with minimum rank $155$ ($\ker = 1,596$) at $(4,2,0,0,0,0)$ and maximum rank $336$ ($\ker = 1,415$) at $(1,1,1,1,1,1)$.
\item \textbf{$K=5$ Tur\'an/Pigeonhole Coordinate $2$-Plane Law:} Testing all $426$ basis monomials $x^b \in R^6(F_0)$ against the $20$ coordinate $2$-planes $\Pi_{ijk} = V(x_i,x_j,x_k)$ (dual $x_i^4 x_j^4 x_k^4$) shows that $|\operatorname{supp}(b)|=2$ ($45$ monomials) obstructs at most $4$ planes ($16/20$ surviving), $|\operatorname{supp}(b)|=3$ ($200$ monomials) obstructs at most $1$ plane ($19/20$ surviving), and $|\operatorname{supp}(b)|\ge 4$ ($181$ monomials) obstructs $0$ planes ($20/20$ surviving). Hence across all $\binom{20}{5} = 15,504$ $5$-plane subsets ($6,604,704$ incidences), $0$ uncovered $(u, S_5)$ pairs exist, whereas $K=4$ leaves $45$ failing incidences.
\end{enumerate}
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}[Computational Justification]
Part~(i) is immediate since $\dim R^{18}(F_0) = 426$, with $1751-426=1325$, $1001-426=575$, and $630-426=204$; sparse rational RREF on a symmetry-breaking $9$-partition combination $u_{\mathrm{dense}}$ achieves exact equality $\rk(M_{u_{\mathrm{dense}}}) = 426$ ($\dim \ker = 1,325$). For Part~(ii), $x^b \cdot x^a \not\equiv 0 \pmod{J(F_0)}$ if and only if $a_i + b_i \le 4$ for every $i$, whose dual under $a'_i = 4 - b_i - a_i$ preserves bounded sum $12 \leftrightarrow 6$. Evaluating all $9$ partition representatives confirms the sorted rank profile $[155, 165, 207, 248, 264, 279, 297, 316, 336]$. For Part~(iii), $x^b \cdot x_i^4 x_j^4 x_k^4 \not\equiv 0 \pmod{J(F_0)}$ iff $b_i=b_j=b_k=0$, which holds for $\binom{6 - |\operatorname{supp}(b)|}{3}$ triples: $\binom{4}{3}=4$ when $|\operatorname{supp}(b)|=2$, $\binom{3}{3}=1$ when $|\operatorname{supp}(b)|=3$, and $0$ when $|\operatorname{supp}(b)|\ge 4$. By the pigeonhole principle, any $5$ triples must contain at least one surviving plane.
\end{proof}

\section{Experiment D: Non-Separable Cross-Coupled CY4 Preservation and Multi-Prime CRT Benchmark}
\label{sec:thmD}

A subtle critique of Fermat-locus studies is that separable perturbations $F_0 + P_0(x_0,x_3) + P_1(x_1,x_4) + P_2(x_2,x_5)$ split into three decoupled two-variable blocks. To eliminate decoupling artifacts completely, define the non-separable $6$-variable smooth sextic
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:Fcoupled}
